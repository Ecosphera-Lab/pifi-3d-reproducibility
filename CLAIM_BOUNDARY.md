# Claim Boundary

This public package is intended to reproduce the finite mathematical claims of the PIFI-3D manuscript.

## Supported claims

- exact geometry-to-grammar derivation for the frozen PIFI carrier;
- primitive terminal periods 8, 12, 12, 20;
- 52-state future-equivalence quotient and resolving depth 13;
- fiber-return reconstruction of the full finite dynamics;
- terminal V4 parity/cocycle finite results;
- voltage/winding census;
- frozen routing and matched-carrier controls;
- a nonempty open two-parameter radial-shell realization chamber with the same labelled rotation system and decorated routed dynamics.

## Not claimed

- physical continuum validity;
- global uniqueness among arbitrary routed-voltage systems;
- that the full domain 0<a<b<1 is one realization chamber;
- novelty of standard voltage/gain graph, finite-state, switching, or graph-cohomological machinery;
- publication priority.
